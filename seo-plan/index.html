Door No. 2 · Search Engine Program

Build the Search Operation Around the Lending Opportunity Faber Already Possesses.

Faber has a valuable funding offer. The company is not currently visible across many of the searches investors use to find that offer. This program builds and manages a coordinated SEO system—market study, competitor monitoring, page improvement, useful content, local visibility, and continuous opportunity identification.

This is not a package of generic blog posts. It is an actively supervised search operation built specifically around Faber Capital Resources.

What We Found (Baseline)

Our independent review of fabercapitalresources.com and the competitive landscape shows a greenfield opportunity—not a ranking war Faber is already mid-fight in.

  • Commercial queries: Faber is effectively unranked against mature hard-money and private-lending competitors on terms investors actually use (e.g. South Florida / Fort Lauderdale private money, fix-and-flip loans Florida).
  • Competitor class: LendingOne, Kiavi, Lima One, Ridge Street, Capital Funding, New Wave, BridgeWell, Easy Street, RBI, and Ohio-market players own the SERPs Faber would need for volume.
  • Technical & content gaps: Missing meta descriptions, weak titles, empty or duplicate URLs, template residue (empty listings, hollow services page), thin portfolio proof, stale blog (2020), and brand/geo mixed signals (South Florida phone vs Cleveland residue).
  • Trust signals: Gmail public email, limited reviews/proof architecture, no clear NMLS/equal-housing presentation on primary pages in the public crawl.

We do not claim guaranteed rankings. We claim a disciplined operating system that improves the probability of being found by the right borrower—continuously.

How We Work the Market

1. Architecture first

Define the primary offer (e.g. South Florida private money / JV for 1–4 unit fix & flip, national by exception), clean conflicting signals, and map the page system: programs, cities, education, proof, and conversion paths.

2. Technical foundation

Indexation hygiene, titles and meta descriptions, sitemap integrity, HTTPS consistency, structured data where appropriate, analytics and Search Console, and removal or consolidation of empty/duplicate pages that dilute the brand.

3. Content that earns trust

Program pages with clear terms language, city pages where Faber actually lends, real case studies (with permission), FAQ schema opportunities, and a content cadence aimed at investor intent—not lifestyle filler.

4. Local and entity

Google Business Profile alignment, NAP consistency, review engine, citations, and a coherent business identity across web and directories.

5. Supervision, not set-and-forget

Recurring audits every two to three days, competitor watches, opportunity flags, and monthly direction you can use with your team.

How This Connects to the Other Doors

SEO without a clear website journey wastes clicks. A strong website without search remains invisible. Automation turns both into an operating system.

  • Website rebuild creates the pages and paths SEO needs to rank and convert.
  • Business automation (Door 2) routes and works the demand SEO and the site generate.
  • SEO program keeps Faber present while competitors publish, advertise, and expand.

Each door is independent. Together they compound.

Realistic Expectations
