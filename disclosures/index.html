Disclosures

This website is a capital-path marketing and education property for Real Estate Capital Resources (RECR). Read these disclosures before relying on any statement about leverage, rates, eligibility, or outcomes.

Not a commitment to lend

Nothing on this site is a commitment to lend, a loan estimate, a LE/CD, or an offer of credit. Any financing is subject to full underwriting, appraisal or valuation as required, clear title, insurance, and final approval by the capital source.

Business-purpose focus

Programs described are intended for business-purpose, investment, and non-owner-occupied real estate scenarios unless expressly stated otherwise. Consumer-purpose primary residence financing is outside the core product set described here.

Claims and numbers

Quantitative marketing claims are governed by an internal claims register. Confirmed mockup figures include up to 100% of purchase, rehab, and closing costs on qualifying joint-venture transactions when total project cost is within 70% of ARV — always with conditions. Other rates, LTVs, fees, volumes, and timelines remain unapproved until documentary sources are filed. Tokens marked confirming are intentionally visible.

Third parties

Capital may be provided by third-party lenders, private capital, or joint-venture partners. RECR may act in a role that is still being finalized for public language; do not assume lender, broker, or advisor status until role language is approved.

Marketing versus underwriting

Website copy describes capital paths at a level useful for investors and partners. Underwriting applies additional rules that may not appear on a marketing page. If a condition matters to your decision, obtain it in writing for your transaction rather than relying on a general web statement.

Forward-looking statements

Any discussion of timelines, market conditions, or project outcomes is forward-looking and uncertain. Illustrative math on program pages is teaching arithmetic, not a projection of your result.
