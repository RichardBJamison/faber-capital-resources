Disclosures

This website is a capital-path marketing and education property for Real Estate Capital Resources (RECR). Read these disclosures before relying on any statement about leverage, rates, eligibility, or outcomes.

Not a commitment to lend

Nothing on this site is a commitment to lend, a loan estimate, a LE/CD, or an offer of credit. Any financing is subject to full underwriting, appraisal or valuation as required, clear title, insurance, and final approval by the capital source.

Business-purpose focus

Programs described are intended for business-purpose, investment, and non-owner-occupied real estate scenarios unless expressly stated otherwise. Consumer-purpose primary residence financing is outside the core product set described here.

Claims and numbers

Quantitative marketing claims are limited to verified figures. Confirmed public conditions include higher-leverage purchase, rehab, and closing options may be available through private-lender programs when deal economics support them — confirmed per transaction — always with conditions. Other rates, LTVs, fees, volumes, and timelines stay unpublished until documentary sources support them.

RECR helps investors identify and pursue the right capital path with capital partners — not automatically the direct funding source or final underwriter — legal role language. Faber Capital Resources operating as Real Estate Capital Resources (RECR) — entity legal name.

No guarantee of outcomes

Past transactions, illustrations, and worked examples are not guarantees of future results. Markets, rates, and underwriting standards change.

Third parties

Capital may be provided by third-party private lenders and capital partners. RECR is positioned as a broker/connector and capital-path guide — not automatically the direct funder. Do not assume lender, broker, or advisor legal status beyond the role language published on this site and in disclosures counsel may update.
