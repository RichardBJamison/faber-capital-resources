## Cannibalization flags

1. **Fix & Flip vs Purchase & Rehab** — comparison URL owns the *vs* query; programs keep head terms.  
2. **Rental vs Multifamily** — rental = coverage structure; multifamily = 2–4 asset type (+ unit friction).  
3. **Bridge vs bridge-to-DSCR** — bridge owns product; bridge-to-dscr owns transition education.  
4. **loan-products vs which-financing-path** — loan-products = catalog; which-path = decision framework.  
5. **Homepage briefings vs programs** — dual layer intentional.  
6. **which-financing-path vs deal-path** — article owns educational comparison SEO; deal-path owns interactive readiness + conversation-path output (not a second article shell).
