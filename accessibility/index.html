Accessibility

RECR aims to make capital-path information usable by the widest practical audience, including people who use assistive technologies.

What we build for

  • Semantic headings and one primary h1 per page
  • Keyboard-operable navigation and flip-card controls
  • Form labels and visible focus states in the shared design system
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images as assets are finalized
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion on header animations

Known limitations (mockup phase)

This property is still under design completion. Some photography may be placeholder; contrast and media alternatives continue to be refined before public launch. Sitewide noindex remains until the launch checklist clears.
