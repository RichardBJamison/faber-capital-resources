What we build for you

Your Deal Launch Kit

One organized package. Known facts, stated assumptions, and a clear list of what a serious buyer still has to verify.

The file

  • Property overview, location, photos, documents
  • Contract price and assignment context
  • Known facts, open questions, items to verify
  • Due-diligence checklist

The numbers

  • Comparable-property context
  • Estimated repair-range assumptions
  • Purchase, rehab, holding, and exit scenarios
  • Buyer-facing deal summary

The share kit

  • Shareable property page and professional PDF
  • Facebook, email, and text-message copy
  • Direct link and QR code
  • Optional financing-review path for buyers

The package is informational. It organizes available information and stated assumptions; it does not guarantee performance, value, sale, or financing.
