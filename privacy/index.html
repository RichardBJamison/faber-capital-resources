Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Real Estate Capital Resources (“RECR,” “we”) handles information collected through this website and deal intake forms. It will be reviewed by counsel before public launch; treat the mockup version as operational intent.

Information we collect

  • Contact data you submit (name, email, phone)
  • Deal data you choose to send (address, prices, budgets, strategy notes)
  • Technical data such as IP, browser, and pages viewed when analytics is enabled
  • Communication records if you email or call us

How we use information

To respond to inquiries, evaluate capital-path fit, improve the site, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Analytics & measurement

Before launch, GA4 (or successor) may be installed with a measurement ID configured in site JavaScript. Event design intends to capture deal_submit, phone_click, email_click, and calculator_complete without sending sensitive borrower document contents to analytics.

Sharing

Deal files may be shared with capital partners, underwriters, and service providers as needed to evaluate or complete a transaction, under confidentiality expectations appropriate to lending workflows.

Retention

We retain inquiry and transaction records as needed for operations, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

Your choices

Request access or deletion of marketing-related personal data by emailing fabercapitalresources@gmail.com. Transaction records may be retained where legally required.

Deal data handling in practice

Property addresses, rent rolls, and financials are sensitive business information. Access is limited to people evaluating or servicing the opportunity. We ask partners and vendors to use the information only for that purpose. Do not send more personal data than the stage of review requires.

Cookies and local storage

The site may use essential storage for form mock status and future preference features. Analytics cookies load only when a real GA4 measurement ID is configured — the placeholder ID does not load Google tags.

Updates

This policy will be updated before public launch after counsel review. Material changes will be reflected by date on this page.
